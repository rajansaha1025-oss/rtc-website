India visa from Bangladesh: how it works

ভারতের ভিসা: কীভাবে আবেদন করবেন

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Check the current rules first

India's visa categories, availability and processing change from time to time. Before you spend money, confirm what is open right now for your visa type. Ask us on WhatsApp or check the official Indian Visa Application Centre (IVAC) website.

Common visa types

  • Tourist: visiting family, sightseeing, short trips.
  • Medical: treatment in India. The patient and attendants apply, with hospital papers.
  • Tirtha (pilgrim): religious travel.
  • Double entry and others: for specific needs; ask us which fits.

Documents people usually need

  • Passport valid for at least 6 months, with blank pages
  • Recent passport-size photos in the required format
  • NID or birth certificate
  • Bank statement and income proof
  • Travel dates and where you will stay
  • Medical visa: a doctor's or hospital letter and patient/attendant details

Your exact list depends on your case. We confirm it before you submit anything.

The usual steps

  1. Choose the correct visa type.
  2. Fill in the online application carefully. Names and dates must match your passport.
  3. Pay the fees and book an appointment at the visa centre.
  4. Visit on your date for document check and biometrics.
  5. Track the application and collect your passport.

Mistakes that cause delays

  • Spelling or date differences between the form and the passport
  • Unclear photos or scans
  • Wrong visa type for the purpose of travel
  • Missing bank or hospital papers
  • Booking travel before the visa is approved

How RTC helps

We check your documents, fill in and review the application, and keep you updated on WhatsApp. You can do the whole process from anywhere in Bangladesh. We cannot guarantee approval; the decision is made by the authority.

Questions

Do I need to visit your Narsingdi office?

No. Send clear photos of your documents on WhatsApp. You only attend the visa-centre appointment yourself when it is required.

Can you guarantee my visa?

No. Approval is decided by the authority. We make sure your application and documents are complete and correct.
