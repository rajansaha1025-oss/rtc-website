Bangladesh e-Passport: new, renewal and re-issue

ই-পাসপোর্ট: নতুন, নবায়ন ও পুনরায় ইস্যু

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Which application is yours?

  • New passport: first time, or no valid passport.
  • Renewal: your passport is expiring or expired.
  • Re-issue: lost or damaged passport.
  • Correction: a mistake in name, date of birth or other details.

Documents people usually need

  • NID or birth certificate (a correct online-registered birth certificate is commonly needed)
  • Passport-size photos where required
  • Old passport, if you have one
  • Address proof and parents' details
  • Re-issue: a general diary (GD) for lost passports

Requirements can change. Confirm your list with us before you start.

The usual steps

  1. Fill in the online application on the official e-passport website.
  2. Choose the passport type, number of pages, validity and delivery speed.
  3. Pay the fee through the approved channel and print the application and payment slip.
  4. Visit the passport office on your appointment for biometrics and document check.
  5. Collect the passport when it is ready, or track it online.

Fees depend on pages, validity and delivery speed. Check the current fee chart on the official site, or ask us.

Mistakes that cause repeat visits

  • Name or date of birth that differs between NID, birth certificate and application
  • Wrong passport type or validity chosen
  • Missing GD or old passport for re-issue
  • Missing the appointment slot

How RTC helps

We check your papers against each other, fill in the application, and tell you exactly what to bring to the passport office so you avoid repeat visits.

Questions

Can I start the application from another district?

Yes, the application and payment are online. Biometrics are done at the passport office you choose.

How long will it take?

It depends on the delivery speed you choose and the office workload. We tell you what to expect after checking your documents.
