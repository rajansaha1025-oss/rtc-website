Common questions

Which visas do you handle?

India (tourist, medical, tirtha, double entry), Malaysia, Australia, Dubai/UAE and other countries.

Do I need to visit the office?

No. Outside Narsingdi you can do everything online: message us on WhatsApp, send photos of your documents, and receive the result by courier or digital copy. In Narsingdi you are welcome at our Kandapara office.

Can you help with ticket date change, refund and cancellation?

Yes. We handle new tickets, date changes, refunds and cancellations. Airline rules and fees apply, and we tell you the exact figure before you decide.

Which other documents do you help with?

NID, TIN certificate, police clearance and BRTA services, plus passport applications and school, college and university admission.

How long does it take?

It depends on the country, the office involved and your case. We give you the expected time when we check your documents, and update you at every step.
