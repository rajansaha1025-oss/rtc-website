Birth and death certificate in Bangladesh

জন্ম ও মৃত্যু নিবন্ধন

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Why the online certificate matters

Birth and death registration in Bangladesh is done through the government's online registration system (BDRIS). Many offices, including passport and NID offices, ask for a correct, online-registered certificate. An error in it can delay every other application.

Birth certificate: documents usually needed

  • Parents' NID or birth certificates
  • Child's hospital card, vaccination card or school certificate
  • Address proof
  • For correction or copy: the existing certificate

Death certificate: documents usually needed

  • Deceased person's NID or birth certificate
  • Proof of death (doctor or hospital certificate, or chairman or councillor letter)
  • Applicant's NID and relationship
  • Address details

Correction

If a name, date or parent's detail is wrong, correct it before you use the certificate for a passport, school or visa. The process and supporting papers depend on what needs fixing.
