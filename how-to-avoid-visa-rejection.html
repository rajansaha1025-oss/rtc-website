How to avoid a visa rejection

ভিসা রিজেক্ট এড়াবেন যেভাবে

Last updated: 16 October 2026

1. Choose the right visa type

Apply for the visa that matches your real purpose: tourism, medical, business, study or pilgrimage.

2. Keep every detail consistent

Name, date of birth, passport number, address and travel dates must match across your form, passport, NID and supporting papers.

3. Use genuine documents only

False or edited documents can lead to refusal and long-term bans. If you are missing a paper, ask us how to get the real one.

4. Show your purpose and ability to return

Provide clear travel dates, accommodation or invitation details, and proof of job, business or family ties and funds that fit the trip.

5. Make financial papers clear

Bank statements should be recent and make sense with your stated income and trip cost. Avoid sudden unexplained deposits.

6. Use clear photos and scans

Blurry, cropped or wrong-size photos and scans cause delays.

7. Apply early and do not book first

Do not buy non-refundable tickets or hotels before the visa is approved. Apply with enough time before travel.

8. Read the latest instructions

Embassies change requirements. Check the official website or ask us for the current checklist before you submit.
