What we handle

What you usually need

  • Passport (valid 6+ months)
  • Passport-size photos
  • NID or birth certificate
  • Bank statement and income proof
  • Travel dates
  • Medical visa: doctor's prescription or hospital letter

Your exact list depends on your case and current rules. We confirm it on WhatsApp before you submit anything.

How it works

1. Message us. Tell us what you need and where you live.
2. Send documents. Photos on WhatsApp, or hand them in at our Narsingdi office.
3. We process and update you. You get the result at the office, by courier or as a digital copy.
