Documents you need / কী কী কাগজ লাগবে

India Tourist Visa

  • Passport (valid 6+ months)
  • Passport-size photos
  • NID or birth certificate
  • Bank statement and income proof
  • Travel dates

India Medical Visa

  • Passport (valid 6+ months)
  • Passport-size photos
  • Doctor's prescription or hospital letter
  • Patient and attendant details
  • Bank statement

India Tirtha (Pilgrim) Visa

  • Passport (valid 6+ months)

  • Travel dates

India Double Entry Visa
