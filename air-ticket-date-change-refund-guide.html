Air ticket date change and refund: what to know

এয়ার টিকেটের তারিখ পরিবর্তন ও রিফান্ড

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Every ticket has fare rules

Whether you can change or refund a ticket, and how much it costs, depends on the airline and the fare you bought. Cheaper fares are often stricter. The rules are shown in the fare conditions at the time of booking.

What usually affects the cost

  • How close to departure you ask for the change
  • The airline's change or cancellation penalty for your fare
  • Any difference between your old fare and the new fare
  • Taxes that the airline does or does not refund
  • The agent's service charge

What to send us

  • Booking reference (PNR) or a photo of the ticket
  • Passenger name exactly as in the passport or NID
  • New date and route, or the reason for cancellation
  • Passport or NID copy

We check the airline rule and tell you the exact cost before you decide.

Tips

  • Ask early: fees usually rise closer to departure.
  • Check passport validity before booking international tickets.
  • Make sure the name spelling matches your passport or NID.
  • Refunds can take time because the airline pays the agent first.

Questions

Is there a fee for a date change?

Usually yes. Airline rules and fees apply. We tell you the exact figure before you confirm.

Can I cancel from outside Narsingdi?

Yes. Send the booking reference on WhatsApp and we handle the rest.
