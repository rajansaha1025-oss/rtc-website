What we handle

  • Visitor visa
  • Student visa

What you usually need

  • Passport (valid 6+ months)
  • Passport-size photos
  • NID or birth certificate
  • Bank statement and income proof
  • Student visa: offer letter and academic papers

Your exact list depends on your case and current rules. We confirm it on WhatsApp before you submit anything.

How it works

1 Message us. Tell us what you need and where you live.
2 Send documents. Photos on WhatsApp, or hand them in at our Narsingdi office.
3 We process and update you. You get the result at the office, by courier or as a digital copy.

Questions

Do you help with student visas?

Yes. We help prepare your documents and application. Admission offers come from the institution.

Can I do everything online?

Yes. Send photos of your documents on WhatsApp and we guide you from there.

How long does it take?

It depends on the visa type and current rules. We share the expected time after checking your documents.
