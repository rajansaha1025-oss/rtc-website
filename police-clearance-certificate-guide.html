Police clearance certificate in Bangladesh

পুলিশ ক্লিয়ারেন্স সার্টিফিকেট

Last updated: 16 October 2026

What it is and when you need it

A police clearance certificate (PCC) shows that police records hold no adverse record against you. It is commonly asked for foreign jobs, some visas, immigration and long stays abroad.

Documents people usually need

  • NID or passport copy
  • Passport-size photos
  • Purpose (job, visa, study, abroad)
  • Present and permanent address details
  • Phone number and email

The embassy or employer sometimes asks for a specific format. Tell us before you apply.

The usual steps

  1. Apply through the official police online service with correct personal details.
  2. Upload documents and photo as required.
  3. Complete any verification step asked of you.
  4. Download or collect the certificate when ready.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Name or date of birth different from the passport
  • Applying with the wrong purpose
  • Waiting until the last minute: some visas need a recent certificate, but processing time varies

Questions

Which certificate does my embassy want?

Check the embassy's instructions and tell us. We help you apply for the right type and format.

Yes, the application is online. Send your documents on WhatsApp and we guide you.
