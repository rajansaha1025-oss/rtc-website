Privacy Policy

Rajan Travel and Computers (RTC), Kandapara, Narsingdi, Bangladesh.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry we collect your name, phone number, the service you need, your location (Narsingdi or elsewhere) and any note you write. When you message us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp's own policy applies to that chat.

Why we collect it

Only to reply to your enquiry and to process the service you ask for. We do not sell your information.

Documents

Passports, ID and other documents you send us are used only for your application and are not shared except with the embassy, airline, office or institution involved in your request.

Where it is stored

Enquiries are stored in a secured Firebase database that only RTC staff can open.

Analytics and ads

We may use Google Analytics, Google Ads and Meta Pixel to understand which pages and ads bring enquiries. These tools may use cookies.
