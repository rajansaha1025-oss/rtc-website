What we do for you

  • NID service (new and correction)
  • TIN certificate
  • Police clearance for jobs, visas and travel abroad
  • BRTA services
  • BMET registration and clearance for working abroad
  • Birth certificate (new, correction, copy)
  • Death certificate

Documents usually needed

  • NID copy or birth certificate
  • Passport-size photos
  • Address details
  • Purpose (for police clearance)
  • Existing licence or vehicle papers (for BRTA)
  • Passport and visa or job papers (for BMET)
  • Parents' NID and hospital or school record (for birth certificate)
  • Deceased person's NID and death proof (for death certificate)

Your exact list depends on your case. We confirm it on WhatsApp.

How it works

1 Message us on WhatsApp, call, or visit the office.
2 Send clear photos of your documents.
3 We check, process and update you at every stage.
4 Collect from our Narsingdi office, or receive by courier or digital copy.

Questions we often hear

Do you need to see my original documents?

Start by sending clear photos on WhatsApp. We tell you if any original is needed for your case.

Can you guarantee approval?

No. Approval is decided by the relevant authority. We make sure your application and documents are complete and correct before you submit.

Can I use your service from outside Narsingdi?

Yes. Send clear photos of your documents on WhatsApp. We guide you step by step and update you at every stage.
